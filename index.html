Writing Challenge #1

"The Last Human & Talking Animals"

"You woke up and realized you are the only human left on Earth, but all animals can suddenly speak English. Write about your first day and your plan."
1. How did you realize you were the last human?
2. Who was the first talking animal you met and what did it say?
3. What is your immediate survival plan?
Writing Challenge #2

"Pass-the-Story (Chain Writing)"

Collaborate with your classmate to build one epic story paragraph by paragraph!

Round 1: Write the beginning of a story (Introduce character, setting & sudden problem).
Round 2: Read your partner's paragraph on screen & write the continuation!
Writing Challenge #3

"Agony Aunt: Teen Hotline"

Give funny, smart, or wise advice to these tricky teen dilemmas!

Problem A: "My best friend is dating my crush, but my crush thinks I wrote them a secret love letter! What should I do?"
Problem B: "I accidentally deleted my teacher's presentation 10 mins before class. Should I confess or blame Wi-Fi?"
